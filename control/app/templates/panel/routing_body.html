Entries with direct sites on

On an entry with this switched on, a site on the lists below is opened from the entry's own address — it sees a Russian visitor — and everything else goes through the hop exactly as before. While the hop is down, listed sites still open and nothing else does. The cost is the point: a listed site sees the entry's address, not the leaf's. Switch it per node on the Nodes screen ("direct sites").
