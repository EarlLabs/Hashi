{# Confirming a revocation. UI-PATTERNS §3: a destructive action is a plain secondary button that confirms in a dialog. The prototype made revoke a toggle with "restore" beside it; this product cannot do that — revoking deletes the friend's key material on every node — and design/README.md says so itself: "if the key cannot be re-minted, irreversible — replace the toggle with a confirm dialog and a disabled row state". The sentence spells out the blast radius rather than asking "are you sure": every node and every protocol, immediately, and not undoable. #}
