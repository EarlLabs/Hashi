` disclosure labelled with its own cost (UI-PATTERNS §6). That is not decoration: this is the form the runbook has you open during a rotation, which is the worst possible moment to read twenty-five labels. The three questions are the three decisions. Everything inside the disclosure is a knob, and every one of those boxes carries what the transport itself says about it — the default it declares, or the shape it will accept when it declares none. Not one of those strings is written here: they arrive in `tuning` from services/transports.py, which reads them out of the transport's own model. That is M9's "no template names a transport, a country, an address or a port" made structural rather than merely checked — there is nothing in this file for a scan to find, because there is nothing in this file to write. NO KEY MATERIAL IS ASKED FOR, and none can be. `tuning` is the transport's required fields MINUS everything it declares as key material, so a hop's private and pre-shared values are absent by construction rather than by omission. The value the two ends must share is minted once for both — services/relay.py, and the transport's own `KeySpec.shared_between_peers` is what says which value that is.
