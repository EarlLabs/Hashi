{# AN ANSWER THAT IS NOT A FAILURE, stated as persistently as one — M22.3. Same slot as the failure banner, same lifetime, same scroll-into-view (static/app.js watches the slot, not what is in it). What differs is the weight and the word: the quieter `.banner` frame described in app.css, and a label that says what happened rather than that something went wrong. IT EXISTS BECAUSE A SUCCESSFUL PROBE WAS INVISIBLE. Acceptance pressed probe four times, waiting 2, 3, 4 and 8 seconds, and never once saw an answer — while every FAILING probe's banner was there at the same waits. The success was a toast, and a toast is cleared after 2200ms (UI-PATTERNS §8), which is shorter than the time it takes to look. An oracle nobody can read is not an oracle. NOT a general licence to answer in a banner. A toast is right for a mutation: the operator did the thing, the thing is done, and the confirmation does not need to survive the next click. A PROBE is not a mutation — it changes nothing, and its answer IS the reason the button was pressed. That is the whole distinction, and the route's docstring (panel/routes.py, probe_node) is where it is argued rather than here. `role="status"` rather than `role="alert"`: this is not an interruption, and a screen reader should hear it after what it was already saying rather than instead of it. #}
