{# Overview — health at a glance. The layout is the design's: four stat cards in a 4-up grid, then a two-up grid of framed preview tables — with one thing above all of it since M28.6, which the design never drew because the product had no backups then: where the backups on this host stand. It is first because it is the only thing on this screen that can be six weeks out of date; panel/overview_body.html says the rest. What the cells CONTAIN is docs/decisions.md decision 6, and the difference is the point of this screen: * "throughput now" is gone — it is a measurement of people, and the charter's non-goals put per-user analytics beyond "off by default": absent. * the Users preview shows `added`, never `last seen`, for the same reason. * the Nodes preview's `load` column is `config`, which reads drift against the digest we last applied. It answers "is this node serving what I told it to", which M1 proved is the question that matters and which nothing in the product could answer at all before this run. The design's "Recent activity" table is deliberately not here, and since M10.3 that is a choice rather than an absence: there IS an audit trail to fill it from now, and no M10 step asks for this card. The omission is not an oversight — it is recorded as one in docs/tracker/M10/step-notes.md, precisely so nobody adds the card back on the assumption it was only ever waiting for the trail. If it is ever built, it shows those entries under the rules decision 8 (docs/decisions.md, 2026-09-11) sets for them: what the operator changed, never who dialled what. #} {% include "panel/console_header.html" %}
