**The test for whether a line belongs here:** *if the next run does not know
this, will it decide wrongly?* If no, it is not a decision — it is progress, and
progress belongs in the task's own files.

Good: "chose Postgres over SQLite — the fleet writes concurrently and SQLite
locks." Bad: "implemented the login screen."

| 2026-09-09 | **The console opts out of htmx's history cache (`hx-history="false"`), and every panel response is `Cache-Control: no-store` with `Vary: HX-Request`.** | Found by chasing an acceptance finding, and the cause was not where anyone looked first. A console URL has **two representations**: the whole document a browser navigates to, and the fragment htmx fetches with `HX-Request: true`. Panel responses carried no cache directive and no `Vary`, so the browser stored the **fragment** under the page's own URL — and a back/forward navigation reuses a stored entry *without revalidating*, rendering a fragment that has no `<head>` as the entire document. That is why the console came back unstyled, why it stayed that way, and why only a reload fixed it: a reload re-requests without `HX-Request`. **It needed a boosted navigation first**, which is exactly why no fresh-load test could see it.
